Try the resume analyzer ↗
How does CareerIQ work?

Paste your resume text into the analyzer and submit it. CareerIQ sends that text to its AI analysis service, which returns skills, strengths, areas to improve, and recommendations.

What happens to my resume data?

Resume text is sent to Groq's API for analysis. Avoid including sensitive information you do not want sent to that external service. CareerIQ does not permanently store the uploaded file.

Can AI guarantee an interview or a specific score?

No. AI feedback can be incomplete or mistaken and is not a hiring decision, guarantee, or substitute for your own judgment. Review suggestions carefully and adapt only what feels accurate to you.

How can I get the most useful feedback?

Include your full resume text with clear sections for experience, skills, and education. Be specific about your role and impact, then use the recommendations as a starting point for your own edits.
